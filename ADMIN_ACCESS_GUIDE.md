## Troubleshooting

### "Can't access /admin"
If you're redirected to the login page when trying to access `/admin`:

1. **Make sure you're signed in** - Look for your email in the header
2. **Try the diagnostic page** - Go to `/admin/access` to check your account status
3. **Use the Fix button** - If the diagnostic shows issues, click "Fix My Profile"

### "Access Denied" message
This means:
- You're not signed in, OR
- Your account doesn't have admin privileges

**Solution**: Go to `/admin/access` and click "Fix My Profile" to grant admin access.

### Still having issues?
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Sign out completely
3. Sign back in with `talktostevenson@gmail.com`
4. Try accessing `/admin` again
